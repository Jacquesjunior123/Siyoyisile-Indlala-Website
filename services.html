Our Services and Programmes

Siyoyisile Indlala Community Farm provides programmes and activities that promote food security, sustainable agriculture and community development.

Community Farming Projects

The organisation promotes community food production through sustainable farming practices. Community members participate in growing vegetables and maintaining food gardens.

Agricultural Training

Training programmes help community members learn farming techniques, crop management, composting, irrigation and sustainable agricultural practices.

Workshops and Education

Educational workshops provide information about food security, environmental sustainability and modern farming methods.

Community Development

The organisation works with local residents, schools and community groups to encourage participation in agricultural projects and food security initiatives.
