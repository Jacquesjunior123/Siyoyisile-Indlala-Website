About Siyoyisile Indlala

Siyoyisile Indlala Community Farm is a grassroots organisation based in Gauteng, South Africa. The organisation focuses on combating hunger and promoting sustainable agricultural practices within the community.

The farm provides a space where community members can learn about farming techniques, share resources and work together to improve food security.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help address food insecurity by encouraging sustainable agriculture, community participation and agricultural education.

Our Vision

Our vision is to support healthy and resilient communities where people have opportunities to grow nutritious food and develop sustainable farming skills.

Our Team

The organisation is supported by people who contribute to community farming, education and sustainable agriculture initiatives.

The team works with community members and partners to encourage food production, knowledge sharing and community development.
