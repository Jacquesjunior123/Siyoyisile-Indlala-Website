Siyoyisile Indlala Community Farm is a community-based organisation in Gauteng that focuses on food security, sustainable agriculture and community development.

Welcome to Siyoyisile Indlala

Siyoyisile Indlala Community Farm works to address food insecurity by promoting sustainable food production, agricultural education and community participation.

The organisation provides opportunities for people to learn about farming and sustainable agricultural practices while contributing to healthier and more resilient communities.

What We Do

Community Farming

We promote food production and sustainable farming practices within the community.

Farming Education

We provide opportunities for community members to learn about agriculture and food production.

Community Development

We encourage community participation and support initiatives that contribute to food security.
